# Privacy Policy — Hair Growth Tracker

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Hair Growth Tracker is a personal tracker for hair treatment routines. This policy explains what
the app stores, what it sends, and what it does not.

## The short version

Your health information never leaves your phone. The app stores your routine, your logged doses,
and your progress photos on the device itself. We cannot see them, and they are never uploaded to
us or anyone else.

## What is stored on your device

All of the following is saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- Your name, and optionally your age and a description of what you're seeing
- The treatments in your routine, their dosages, and their schedules
- Every dose you log as taken or not taken
- Your progress photos
- Your app settings, such as whether reminders are on

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to our servers, and we have no way to
access it.

## What is sent from your device

One thing. When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous account with our backend
provider, Supabase. This account contains no personal information — no name, no email, no health
data. It exists only so that features requiring a server can be added later without asking you to
create an account for something you already have.

As with any internet request, our backend provider receives and may log technical information
such as your IP address and the time of the request. This is standard server operation, is not
used to identify or track you, and is not combined with anything else.

## What the app does not do

- It does not use analytics or tracking of any kind
- It does not show advertising
- It does not sell or share your information with anyone
- It does not access your contacts, location, or microphone

## Permissions

- **Camera** — used only to take your progress photos, which are saved to your phone. The app
  never uploads them.
- **Notifications** — used only to remind you about doses you have scheduled. Reminders are
  created on your device; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

## Sharing your own data

The app can produce a PDF summary of your consistency and photos, intended for showing a doctor.
This is created on your phone and only shared if you choose to share it, to a destination you
pick. We never receive a copy.

## Backups

Android may back up a small amount of app data — your routine, your logged doses, and your first
set of photos — to your own Google Drive, so it can be restored if you reinstall. This is a
service between you and Google. We have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's
backup settings.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything stored on your device. To delete the
anonymous account record, contact us at the address below.

## Children

Hair Growth Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
Material changes affecting what leaves your device will also be surfaced in the app.
